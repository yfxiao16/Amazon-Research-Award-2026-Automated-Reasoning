\section{Expected results}

The plan for the first year is organized by quarter, each ending with a research result and a deliverable.
{\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{1.25cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Months} & \textbf{Research result} & \textbf{Deliverable} \\
\midrule
1--3 & Decomposition protocol; sound GR(1) quotient approximations (Thrust 1) & Technical report; \chase release with MCP server and agent skill \\
4--6 & Circular \gls{ag} rule for safety guarantees with a soundness proof; generalization of unrealizable cores (Thrust 1) & Open-source hardware flow with SymbiYosys on AWS; RTL benchmark with faulty variants \\
7--9 & Quotient-based delegation and checked compaction over \gls{ltlf} (Thrust 2); quotient-based repair and certificate reuse (Thrust 3) & Hardware and multi-robot evaluation; conference paper \\
10--12 & Choice between one agent and several (Thrust 2); contract coverage (Thrust 3) & Evaluation on AppWorld; tool paper on \chase for agents; presentation to Amazon teams \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
}

Beyond the first year, we expect these results to extend to decidability results for further classes of architectures and to contracts with quantitative and probabilistic guarantees. All code, benchmarks, and run traces will be released under open-source licenses.
