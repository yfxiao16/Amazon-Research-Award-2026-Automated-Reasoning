\section{Additional information}

\textbf{1. Application domain and analysis techniques.} Compositional design of hardware, multi-robot systems, and LLM agents, using \gls{ag} contracts, \gls{ltl} and \gls{ltlf} model checking (nuXmv), \gls{smt} reasoning, GR(1) synthesis (slugs), and RTL property verification (Jasper, SymbiYosys).

\textbf{2. Current applications.} \chase has been used for requirement formalization and validation of cyber-physical systems~\cite{nuzzo2018chase}, contract-based architecture exploration~\cite{xiao2024contrarc,xiao2026smcp}, and consistency and availability analysis of distributed systems in collaboration with Lockheed Martin~\cite{xiao2026memocode}. Our RTL flow runs on OpenTitan and HierSVA designs.

\textbf{3. Potential applications to Amazon.} Verification of AWS silicon; controllers for Amazon Robotics fleets; multi-agent workflows on Amazon Bedrock whose obligations are preserved across delegation, at lower serving cost; and consistency and vacuity analysis of formalized policies, complementary to Automated Reasoning checks in Bedrock Guardrails.

\textbf{4--5. Prior Amazon Research Awards.} No. The project team has not received Amazon cash funding or AWS Promotional Credits in the past three years.

\textbf{6. Assumptions and soundness.} A certified result is sound when (i) the back ends return correct verdicts; (ii) the translation into each back end preserves the semantics of the contracts; (iii) every composition step is acyclic or satisfies the side condition of the circular rule in Thrust~1; and (iv) the top-level specification is correct, which is why agent-written contracts are checked for consistency and vacuity. Bounded results are never counted as proofs, LLM outputs are never trusted, and quotient approximations are used only in the direction that preserves refinement.

\textbf{7. Tools.} \emph{License:} BSD 3-Clause for \chase and its MCP server, Apache 2.0 for the RTL flow, and MIT for ACIES. \emph{On-boarding:} a website with a usage guide and API reference, a Docker image, and the agent skill; a tutorial will be added. \emph{Maintenance:} over 90 commits in the last three months; one active contributor.
