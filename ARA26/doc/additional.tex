\section{Additional information}

\textbf{1. Application domain and analysis techniques.} Compositional design of hardware, multi-robot systems, and distributed systems, using \gls{ag} contracts, \gls{ltl} model checking (nuXmv), \gls{smt} reasoning, GR(1) synthesis (slugs), hardware model checking (SymbiYosys, Jasper), and mixed-integer linear programming (Gurobi). It covers sound neurosymbolic reasoning, LLM--solver interfaces, vacuity and inconsistency detection, formal models of hardware, and distributed systems.

\textbf{2. Current applications.} \chase has been used for requirement formalization and validation of cyber-physical systems~\cite{nuzzo2018chase}, contract-based architecture exploration~\cite{xiao2024contrarc,xiao2026smcp}, and consistency and availability analysis of distributed systems with Lockheed Martin~\cite{xiao2026memocode}. Our RTL flow runs on OpenTitan and HierSVA designs.

\textbf{3. Potential applications to Amazon.} (i) Verification of AWS processors and accelerators, where end-to-end properties of interconnects and memory subsystems beyond the capacity of direct model checking can be decomposed into module-level SVA contracts and reused when a block is resized. (ii) Design of distributed cloud services, where availability and consistency requirements can be decomposed into per-component timing contracts~\cite{xiao2026memocode}. (iii) Controller design for Amazon Robotics fleets, where missions can be decomposed into GR(1) contracts and synthesized into monitored controllers~\cite{xiao2026acies}.

\textbf{4--5. Prior Amazon Research Awards.} No, and no Amazon funding or AWS credits in the past three years.

\textbf{6. Assumptions and soundness.} A certified result is sound when (i) the back ends return correct verdicts; (ii) the translation into each back end preserves the semantics of the contracts; (iii) every composition step is acyclic or satisfies the side condition of the circular rule in Thrust~1; and (iv) the top-level specification is correct, which is why agent-written contracts are checked for consistency and vacuity. Bounded results never count as proofs, LLM outputs are never trusted, and quotient approximations only strengthen guarantees.
