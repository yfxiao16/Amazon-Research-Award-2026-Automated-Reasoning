\section{Additional information}

\textbf{1. Application domain and analysis techniques.} Compositional verification and design of hardware, multi-robot systems, and LLM agents, using \gls{ag} contracts, \gls{ltl} and \gls{ltlf} model checking (nuXmv), \gls{smt} reasoning, GR(1) synthesis (slugs), and RTL property verification (Jasper, SymbiYosys).

\textbf{2. Current applications.} \chase has been used for requirement formalization and validation of cyber-physical systems~\cite{nuzzo2018chase}, contract-based architecture exploration~\cite{xiao2024contrarc,xiao2026smcp}, and consistency and availability analysis of distributed systems in collaboration with Lockheed Martin~\cite{xiao2026memocode}. Our RTL flow runs on OpenTitan, HierSVA, and riscv-formal designs; ACIES runs on multi-robot warehouse and charging tasks.

\textbf{3. Potential applications to Amazon.} Verification of AWS silicon, where end-to-end properties span many modules; controllers with guarantees for Amazon Robotics fleets sharing chargers, aisles, and docks; multi-agent workflows on Amazon Bedrock in which the obligations of each agent are preserved across delegation; and consistency and vacuity analysis of formalized policies, complementary to Automated Reasoning checks in Amazon Bedrock Guardrails.

\textbf{4--5. Prior Amazon Research Awards.} The PI has not received an Amazon Research Award, and the project team has not received cash funding or AWS Promotional Credits from Amazon in the past three years.

\textbf{6. Assumptions and soundness.} A certified result is sound when (i) the back ends return correct verdicts; (ii) the translation of contracts into each back end preserves their semantics, which we ensure by restricting SystemVerilog assertions to an LTL-expressible subset; (iii) every composition step is acyclic or satisfies the side condition of the circular rule in Thrust~1; and (iv) the top-level specification is correct, which is why extracted contracts are checked for consistency and vacuity and confirmed by the user. Bounded results are never counted as proofs, and LLM outputs are never part of the trusted base. Quotient approximations are used only in the direction that preserves refinement.

\textbf{7. Tools.} \emph{License:} \chase and the \chase MCP server are released under the BSD 3-Clause license, the RTL verification flow under Apache 2.0, and ACIES under the MIT license. \emph{On-boarding material:} a website with a usage guide and an API reference, a Docker image with all the solvers, and the agent skill with worked examples; a tutorial will be added. \emph{Maintenance:} \chase is actively maintained, with over 90 commits and continuous integration in the last three months. It has one active contributor.
