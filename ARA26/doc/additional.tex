\section{Additional information}

\textbf{1. Application domain and analysis techniques.} Compositional design of hardware and multi-robot systems, with multi-agent LLM workflows as an additional example, using \gls{ag} contracts, \gls{ltl} and \gls{ltlf} model checking (nuXmv), \gls{smt} reasoning, GR(1) synthesis (slugs), and hardware model checking (e.g., SymbiYosys). It addresses the call topics on sound neurosymbolic reasoning, LLM--solver interfaces, vacuity and inconsistency detection, and formal models of hardware.

\textbf{2. Current applications.} \chase has been used for requirement formalization and validation of cyber-physical systems~\cite{nuzzo2018chase}, contract-based architecture exploration~\cite{xiao2024contrarc,xiao2026smcp}, and consistency and availability analysis of distributed systems in collaboration with Lockheed Martin~\cite{xiao2026memocode}. Our SMC solver~\cite{shoukry2018smc} has also enabled counterexample-guided repair of neural networks~\cite{fayyazi2025fairness}. Our RTL flow runs on OpenTitan and HierSVA designs.

\textbf{3. Potential applications to Amazon.} Verification of AWS silicon; controllers for Amazon Robotics fleets; design of multi-agent workflows on Amazon Bedrock whose decomposition into roles is certified before deployment, at lower serving cost; and consistency and vacuity analysis of formalized policies, complementary to Automated Reasoning checks in Bedrock Guardrails.

\textbf{4--5. Prior Amazon Research Awards.} No; the project team has received no Amazon funding or AWS credits in the past three years.

\textbf{6. Assumptions and soundness.} A certified result is sound when (i) the back ends return correct verdicts; (ii) the translation into each back end preserves the semantics of the contracts; (iii) every composition step is acyclic or satisfies the side condition of the circular rule in Thrust~1; and (iv) the top-level specification is correct, which is why agent-written contracts are checked for consistency and vacuity. Bounded results never count as proofs, LLM outputs are never trusted, and quotient approximations only strengthen guarantees.

\textbf{7. Tools.} \emph{License:} BSD 3-Clause for \chase and its MCP server, Apache 2.0 for the RTL flow, and MIT for ACIES. \emph{On-boarding:} a website with a usage guide and API reference, a Docker image, and the agent skill; a tutorial is planned. \emph{Maintenance:} maintained by our lab; over 90 commits in three months.
